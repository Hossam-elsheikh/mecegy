MEC Partners with the Egyptian Engineers Syndicate to Empower Future Civil Engineers 🔧👷
We at Middle East Engineering Consultants (MEC) were thrilled to be one of the key sponsors of the employment fair organized by the Civil Engineering Division and the Employment Committee of the Egyptian Engineers Syndicate , as part of Civil Engineers' Day celebrations.

The event, attended by Eng. Tarek El-Nabrawy , head of the syndicate, highlighted the importance of continuous learning and skill development for young engineers. MEC received many CVs, offered job vacancies, and distributed valuable giveaways to participants—just the beginning of our commitment to supporting the future of civil engineering in Egypt.

Our CEO Eng. Khaled Abdelgaber, Chairman Prof. Dr. Hesham Diab, and our engineering team were present to ensure direct communication with upper management and young talents.

A 1,900 meter-long and 16.70-meter-wide bridge spans the Cairo-Aswan railway lines and the Ibrahimia Canal, aiming to alleviate traffic congestion and enhance regional connectivity. 

Prof. Diab, a Professor of Concrete Structures at Assiut University, brings extensive expertise in reinforced concrete design to this project.
